About Prof. Rajanikant A. Metri

Prof. Rajanikant A. Metri is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Rajarambapu Institute of Technology. His professional journey combines engineering practice, teaching, research, student project development, technical workshops and academic service.

His academic work spans control engineering, piecewise-smooth and discontinuous dynamical systems, power electronics, microgrids, renewable-energy systems, industrial automation, PLC-SCADA, electric vehicles, AI/ML applications and engineering education.
