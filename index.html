01 · ABOUT

Teaching, research and engineering practice.

Prof. Rajanikant A. Metri is an Assistant Professor at Rajarambapu Institute of Technology. His academic profile combines teaching, research, project development, technical workshops and industry experience in electrical engineering.

His work spans control engineering, piecewise-smooth dynamical systems, power electronics, DC microgrids, renewable-energy systems, industrial automation, PLC-SCADA, electric vehicles and AI/ML-enabled energy applications.

This website presents selected professional information from the current CV. Personal residential details are intentionally not published.
